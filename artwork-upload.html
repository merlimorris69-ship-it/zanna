Artwork Guidelines & File Upload

Follow our simple pre-flight specifications to ensure your printed product emerges with crisp sharp details, accurate colors, and immaculate trimming.

Accepted File Formats

Our prepress rip software prefers vector and high-resolution production master files:

  • PDF: Print-Ready PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 (Recommended)
  • AI / EPS: Adobe Illustrator with all fonts outlined
  • PSD / TIFF: Adobe Photoshop with flattened layers
  • JPG / PNG: High-res export at 100% size

Resolution Standards

Images must possess sufficient pixel density to avoid visible blurriness:

  • Small & Medium Print: 300 DPI at 100% final scale (Business cards, flyers, stickers)
  • Large Format Signs: 150 DPI at 100% (Vinyl banners, billboards)
  • Web Images (72 DPI): Do not use low-res screenshots or WhatsApp compressed photos

CMYK Color Mode

Digital monitors display RGB (light-emitting), while printers utilize CMYK (cyan, magenta, yellow, black) ink pigments:

  • Always design in CMYK Color Mode (FOGRA39 or SWOP profile)
  • Avoid neon electric RGB values which naturally shift slightly darker in physical ink
  • For rich deep black backgrounds, use: C:40 M:30 Y:30 K:100

Bleed & Margins

Guillotine cutting requires a small boundary margin to prevent unwanted white slivers:

  • Add 3mm (0.125") bleed on all 4 sides
  • Extend background colors & photos fully into the bleed
  • Keep critical text & logos at least 5mm inside the cut trim line
