Our Roots & Journey

Built on Local Trust, Powered by Industrial Precision

Founded in Unnappilavu, Mullaitivu, Merlimorris Printing Shop was established with a singular vision: to bring metropolitan-grade digital, offset, and wide-format printing right to our local community without long shipping delays or high transport overheads.

Today, our facility operates around the clock — running a continuous 24 hours a day, 6 days a week (24/6) schedule. Whether a small family cafe needs morning menus or a multinational event requires fifty 20-foot vinyl banners overnight, our presses never sleep.
